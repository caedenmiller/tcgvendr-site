30th Celebration Giveaway: Official Rules

Effective October 5, 2026

These are the official rules for the TCGVendr 30th Celebration Elite Trainer Box giveaway on Instagram and TikTok. No purchase is necessary to enter or win, and buying anything won’t improve your chances.

  2. Dates
  3. Who can enter
  4. How to enter
  5. The prize
  6. Picking the winner
  7. If you win
  8. Fair play
  9. Release
  10. Your information

1. Who’s running it

The giveaway is run by TCGVendr (“we”), the maker of the TCGVendr app, through @tcgvendr.app on Instagram and TikTok. Questions go to support@tcgvendr.com.

This giveaway is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by or associated with Instagram, Meta, TikTok, Apple or The Pokémon Company.

2. Dates

  • Opens when the giveaway post goes up on each account, on October 5, 2026.
  • Closes Sunday, October 11, 2026 at 11:59 PM Eastern Time (4:59 AM UK time on Monday, October 12). Entries after that don’t count.
  • Winner picked Monday, October 12, 2026.

3. Who can enter

  • Residents of the 50 United States, Washington, D.C. and the United Kingdom.
  • You need your own Instagram or TikTok account, which means meeting that platform’s own age rules.
  • If you’re under 18, you need a parent or guardian’s permission to enter. If a minor wins, the prize is sent to their parent or guardian.
  • One prize per household.
  • The people running the giveaway and their households can’t enter.
  • Void where prohibited.

4. How to enter

It works the same way on Instagram and TikTok:

  • Follow @tcgvendr.app.
  • Like the giveaway post.
  • Tag a friend in a comment on the giveaway post. Each comment that tags a different friend is 1 entry, up to 10 entries per platform. Tag real people. Tagging the same friend again, brand pages or fake accounts doesn’t count.

Bonus entries:

  • +5: get the free TCGVendr app (iPhone) from tcgvendr.com and comment your TCGVendr username on the giveaway post. New and existing accounts both count. The +5 counts once per person and once per TCGVendr account, even if you comment it on both platforms.
  • +3: repost the giveaway post or share it to your story, once per platform. We can only count the reposts and shares we can see, so a share from a private account may not count.

Instagram and TikTok entries go into the same draw. You can enter on both, and each platform’s entries count on their own.

5. The prize

One winner gets one Pokémon TCG: 30th Celebration Elite Trainer Box, English, factory sealed. Approximate value: $155 USD (its market value on October 2, 2026).

We pay shipping to the winner’s address in the US or UK. The prize can’t be swapped for cash or transferred. If the box becomes unavailable, we may substitute a prize of equal or greater value.

6. Picking the winner

On Monday, October 12, 2026 we pick one winner at random from every eligible entry on both platforms, using a random-selection tool, and we record the draw on screen. Your odds depend on how many eligible entries come in.

Before we announce, we check that the winner follows @tcgvendr.app and liked the giveaway post on the platform they entered on. If they don’t, or they aren’t eligible, we draw again. We announce the winner by username on both accounts.

7. If you win

  • We’ll message you from @tcgvendr.app on the platform you entered on. You have 48 hours to reply with your shipping address, or we draw another winner.
  • We will never ask you for payment, a password or a login code. Anyone else who messages you about this giveaway is a scam, so please report them.

8. Fair play

We can disqualify entries or entrants that use bots, fake or duplicate accounts, bought engagement, or anything else that breaks these rules or Instagram’s or TikTok’s rules. If something outside our control affects the giveaway, such as a platform outage or deleted comments, we’ll do what’s fair to everyone who entered.

9. Release

By entering, you agree to these rules. You also release Instagram, Meta and TikTok from any liability connected with this giveaway, and you acknowledge that the giveaway is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by or associated with them.

If you live in the UK, nothing in these rules affects your statutory rights.

10. Your information

Your comments are public on the platform where you post them. We use the winner’s name and shipping address only to send the prize, and we delete them once it’s delivered. Entering doesn’t sign you up for anything. The Privacy Policy covers the app itself.
