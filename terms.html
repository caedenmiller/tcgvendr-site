1. What TCGVendr is, and what it isn’t

TCGVendr helps people find cards at trading-card shows. Vendors publish the cards they plan to bring to a show, collectors track their collections and can share the cards they’re bringing, and anyone can browse, favorite and message.

TCGVendr is not a marketplace. You can’t buy or sell a card in the app, and there’s no cart, payment, shipping or escrow. Any deal you arrange happens in person, at your own judgment, directly with the other person. We aren’t a party to it and aren’t responsible for it. The only thing you can pay for in the app is a Pro subscription (section 8).

Prices in the app are either prices a person typed in or market values from Scrydex, our catalog and pricing provider. Market values come from sales and listings elsewhere, and they can be wrong or out of date. A card is worth what someone will actually pay for it. Treat every number in the app as information, not as an appraisal or investment advice.

8. TCGVendr Pro

TCGVendr is free to download and use. TCGVendr Pro is an optional, auto-renewing subscription that adds:

  • Full price history: every range on every card’s price graph. Without Pro, graphs cover up to one month.
  • Unlimited scanning. Without Pro you get 25 card scans and 10 slab scans in any rolling seven days. A slab counts as a slab scan in any scan mode.
  • Real card photos: photos of your own cards in place of the catalog art.
  • Real slab photos: PSA’s own photo on every PSA slab you add. Without Pro, your first 10 PSA slabs get one and the rest show a drawn slab.
  • Market insights: your full list of top movers, plus sell-through, profit and ROI on your sales.
  • The Pro badge on your profile and your tables.

The subscription terms:

  • Price. $7.99 a month or $44.99 a year in the US. Apple sets prices in other regions and shows them at checkout.
  • Free trial. New subscribers get 7 days free. Apple allows one free trial per Apple ID. If you cancel before the trial ends, you aren’t charged.
  • Billing. Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase. We never see or handle your card details.
  • Renewal. The subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your Apple ID is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Managing it. Turn off auto-renew or cancel any time in iOS Settings under your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and you keep Pro until the paid period runs out.
  • Refunds. Apple handles purchases, so refund requests go to Apple.
  • Restoring. A subscription belongs to the Apple ID that bought it. “Restore purchases” in the app brings it back on another device.

Pro gifts. Sometimes we give Pro at no charge. A gift never turns into a paid subscription and never charges you.

If Pro ends for any reason, your account and your whole collection stay put. Only the Pro features switch off.
