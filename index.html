Common questions.

Is this a marketplace?

No. Vendors post what they’re bringing and the deal happens in person at the show. There are no payments, no shipping and no escrow in the app.

Do I need an account?

Not to look around. Browsing shows, tables and cards works without one, and so does saving them for later. You’ll need an account to publish a table, tap Interested, or message someone.

Which card games are supported?

Pokémon right now, English and Japanese sets plus sealed product. The catalog was built to take Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh!, One Piece and Lorcana later.

Where do the prices come from?

Scrydex, refreshed daily. Graded cards are priced off recent completed sales, and those sales are listed under the chart so you can check the math. Vendors can take the market price, set their own, or leave a card as Ask.

I run a show. Can I get it listed?

Yes. Submit it from the Shows tab. Submissions get reviewed before they publish, and listing is free.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. iOS is live now and Android is planned.
