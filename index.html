Questions

Something else? Email support@tcgvendr.com

Is TCGVendr a marketplace?

No. Vendors show what they’re bringing, and you buy in person at the show. There are no payments, shipping or escrow in the app.

Is it free?

Yes. Shows, tables, search, your collection, favorites, messages and publishing a table are all free, and you get 25 card scans and 10 slab scans a week. TCGVendr Pro is optional.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Sign in with Apple, Google or your email (we send you a code, there’s no password). Then add a display name and your home state so the app can show you shows nearby.

Who can see my collection?

Your cards show on your profile, with an asking price if you set one. Nobody sees what you paid or what you’ve sold. Hide any card to take it off your profile.

Where do the prices come from?

Raw cards and sealed follow the live market and update every day. Slabs are priced from recent sales, and you can see those sales under the chart. On a table, the vendor’s own price comes first.

Which games does it cover?

Pokémon for now: English and Japanese cards, plus sealed product. Japanese cards show their English names.

I run a show. How do I get it listed?

Tap + on the Shows tab and fill it in. We check every show before it goes live, and listing is free.

Is there an Android app?

Not yet. TCGVendr is iPhone only for now. On an Android phone, tap Get notified anywhere on this page and leave your email, and we’ll tell you the day it’s out.
