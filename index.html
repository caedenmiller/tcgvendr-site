FAQ

Common questions.

Is TCGVendr a marketplace?

No. Sellers preview what they're bringing and deals happen in person at the show. There are no payments, shipping, or escrow in the app.

Which card games are supported?

Pokémon (English) at launch. The catalog is built to add Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh!, One Piece, and Lorcana later.

How do I get the beta?

Email support@tcgvendr.com for a TestFlight invite. iOS is live now; Android is planned.

Where do prices come from?

Market data refreshed daily, covering raw conditions, grades, and variants. Sellers can use the market price, set their own, or leave a card as "Ask."

I run a show — can I get it listed?

Yes. Submit it from the Shows tab. Submissions are reviewed before publication.
