FAQ

Quick answers.

Is TCGVendr a marketplace?

No — it's discovery. Sellers publish previews of what they're bringing; you browse, search, and chat. There are no payments, shipping, or escrow in the app. Every deal happens in person, at the table.

Which card games are supported?

Pokémon (English) is the launch focus. The catalog architecture is built multi-TCG, so Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh!, One Piece, and Lorcana can follow as the network grows.

How do I get the beta?

Email support@tcgvendr.com and we'll send you a TestFlight invite. iOS is live now; Android is planned — the app is built from one codebase for both.

Where do prices come from?

Live market data, refreshed daily, covering raw conditions, grades, and variants. Sellers can keep the market price, set their own sticker price, or leave a card as "Ask."

I run a show — can I get it listed?

Yes. Anyone can submit a show from the Shows tab; submissions are reviewed before going public so the calendar stays clean. Organizer-managed show pages are on the roadmap.
