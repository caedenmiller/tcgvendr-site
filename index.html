Price history

Years of sales history
in one graph.

Millions of real datapoints for singles, slabs and sealed.

  • Graded prices come from completed sales: the ten most recent, weighted toward the newest.
  • Those exact sales sit under the chart, dated and linked, so you can check the number yourself.
  • PSA population counts for every grade, right beside the price.
  • When there are no sales to go on, it says so instead of quietly guessing.

For collectors

Know what your
collection is worth.

Daily portfolio tracking, market analytics and card trends.

  • Everything you own gets repriced daily, singles and sealed on the same footing.
  • What you paid is tracked per purchase, so the profit figure is the real one.
  • One notification a day, and only when the value actually moved. It names the card that moved most.
  • Nothing is public unless you publish it. Any card can be hidden.

Scan & search

Or a whole page.

Point the camera at a binder page and every card in it comes back identified and priced.

  • Slabs come back graded, with the company and the grade already filled in.
  • The whole Pokémon catalog, down to the printing, English and Japanese.
  • Japanese cards read in English everywhere in the app, tagged JP so you know what you're holding.
  • 25 scans a week on the free app. Pro takes the cap off.

Match

to trade with.

Show interest in someone's inventory to kickstart a deal.

  • Tap Interested on any card in anyone's collection.

  • You can block anyone, and they disappear from your side of the app entirely.

Common questions.

Is this a marketplace?

No. Vendors post what they're bringing and the deal happens in person at the show. There are no payments, no shipping and no escrow in the app.

Do I need an account?

Not to look around. Browsing shows, tables and cards works without one, and so does saving them for later. You'll need an account to publish a table, tap Interested, or message someone.

Which card games are supported?

Pokémon right now, English and Japanese sets plus sealed product. The catalog was built to take Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh!, One Piece and Lorcana later.

Where do the prices come from?

Scrydex, refreshed daily. Graded cards are priced off recent completed sales, and those sales are listed under the chart so you can check the math. Vendors can take the market price, set their own, or leave a card as Ask.

I run a show. Can I get it listed?

Yes. Submit it from the Shows tab. Submissions get reviewed before they publish, and listing is free.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. iOS is live now and Android is planned.
